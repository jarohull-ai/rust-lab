# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in Rust Lab welcoming and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of experience, education, identity, disability, appearance, nationality, or background.
Learning questions are welcome. Technical disagreement must remain about the work, never the person.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections.
- Give actionable feedback and credit other people's work.
- Protect private information and report security concerns privately.
- Help keep discussions relevant and accessible to learners.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, repeated disruption, or publishing another person's private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and contributions that violate these
standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should
be proportionate, documented privately, and protect reporters from retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not use a
public issue when doing so would expose a target or reporter.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing Rust Lab.
